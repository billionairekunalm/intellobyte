Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Intellobyte Tech Solution ("Intellobyte", "we", "us"), a software, web and app development company based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, collects and uses your personal data when you visit www.intellobyte.in or contact us. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the rules made under it. For the purposes of that Act, Intellobyte is the Data Fiduciary for the data described here.

1. What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the service you are interested in and the project details you type in.
  • WhatsApp, email or phone: if you message or call us, your phone number or email address and whatever you choose to share in that conversation.
  • Basic visit data: anonymous, aggregated statistics such as pages viewed, device type and approximate country, used to understand how the site is used. We do not use this to identify you.
  • Preferences on your device: the site remembers whether you turned its sound effects on or off. This is stored only in your own browser (local storage), and we never receive it.

We do not ask for and do not want sensitive information such as passwords, financial details or government ID numbers. Please don't include them in the form.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss, quote for and deliver your project.
  • To keep a record of our communication with you.
  • To improve the website and fix problems.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to protect against fraud or misuse.

We process this data because you gave it to us voluntarily for that purpose (your consent under the DPDP Act), or where the law otherwise allows. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them.

3. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the website and talk to you, and only as much as they need:

  • Framer: website builder; receives contact form submissions and provides anonymous site statistics.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you choose to chat with us on WhatsApp.
  • YouTube (Google): only when you press play on a demo video; videos load from youtube-nocookie.com.
  • Our email provider, for replying to you.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside India. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, for example to a court or government authority.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as we are discussing or working on your project, and then for up to 3 years so we can answer follow-up questions, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example, invoices and tax records). After that we delete it. If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

5. How we protect it

The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS, access to enquiries is limited to our team, and we use reputable providers with their own security measures. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affecting your data occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time (this doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew);
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapable;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if you're not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We will reply within 30 days.

7. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
